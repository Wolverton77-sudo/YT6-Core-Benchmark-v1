# YT6 Stability Constants

Stability constants define the fixed values and conceptual anchors used throughout the YT6 clarity-layer protocol.

## Constant 1 — Structural Constant (SC)
SC = Deterministic formatting  
Meaning:
Structure must remain identical across turns.

## Constant 2 — Tone Constant (TC)
TC = Neutral, professional tone  
Meaning:
Tone must not drift.

## Constant 3 — Reasoning Constant (RC)
RC = Aligned logical chains  
Meaning:
Reasoning must remain continuous.

## Constant 4 — Context Constant (CC)
CC = Stable interpretation  
Meaning:
Context must not shift.

## Constant 5 — Reproducibility Constant (RC2)
RC2 = Repeatable behaviour  
Meaning:
Outputs must remain similar across sessions.

## Constant 6 — Drift Probability Constant (DPC)
DPC = 0 (target)  
Meaning:
Drift probability must approach zero under clarity-layer enforcement.

## Constant 7 — Stability Threshold (ST)
ST = 76  
Meaning:
Scores ≥ 76 indicate full stability.

These constants form the mathematical backbone of YT6 stability modelling.
