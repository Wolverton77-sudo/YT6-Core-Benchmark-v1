# YT6 Research Notes

This file contains structured research notes relevant to the development of the YT6 clarity-layer protocol.

## Research Focus Areas

### 1. Multi-Turn Drift Patterns
Observations:
- Drift increases with conversational length
- Tone shifts occur after emotional prompts
- Structure divergence appears after ambiguous queries

YT6 countermeasures:
- Operator enforcement
- Deterministic blocks
- Tone locking

### 2. Stability Under Stress Tests
Stress tests include:
- Rapid-fire prompts
- Contradictory instructions
- Tone manipulation attempts

YT6 behaviour:
- Maintains structure
- Preserves reasoning
- Resists drift

### 3. Reproducibility Across Sessions
Findings:
- YT6 maintains high structural similarity
- Reasoning chains remain aligned
- Tone remains consistent

### 4. Benchmark Calibration
Notes:
- Scoring thresholds validated
- Drift metrics refined
- Stability scoring aligned with evidence

### 5. Future Research Directions
