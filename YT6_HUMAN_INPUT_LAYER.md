# YT6 — Human Input Layer
## Why YT6 Cannot Work Without the Average Human

YT6 is a clarity protocol, not a generator or controller.  
It only activates when a human speaks first.  
Without human input, there is no translation to perform and no clarity to produce.

---

## 1. The Human Is the Source, Not the Target

YT6 assumes:
- the human brings intent  
- the human brings words  
- the human brings “clumsy” shorthand  
- the human brings their own rules  

YT6 does not replace this.  
It **receives** it and turns it into structured, drift‑free interaction.

No human → no intent → no input → no YT6.

---

## 2. YT6 as a Translator, Not a Controller

YT6:
- does not control humans  
- does not force behaviour  
- does not impose rules  
- does not guarantee outcomes  

YT6:
- translates human intent  
- stabilises messy input  
- removes drift and padding  
- structures tasks for execution  

It is a **translation layer**, not a rule engine.

---

## 3. Imperfect Input Is the Fuel

Average humans bring:
- incomplete thoughts  
- mixed metaphors  
- shorthand like `x = c2(+xdf)y.3`  
- emotional framing  
- non‑academic language  

YT6 uses this as raw material.

Each iteration:
- cleans the input  
- clarifies the intent  
- structures the task  
- returns a clearer output  

The system improves **because** the human is imperfect.

---

## 4. The 60/40 Ratio Depends on Human Input

YT6 enforces:
- **Human: 60% (lead)**  
- **AI: 40% (support)**  

This ratio only exists if:
- the human provides the 60%  
- the AI responds with the 40%

Without human input, the ratio collapses.  
YT6 is designed to keep the human in charge.

---

## 5. Backend Maths and Code, Frontend Human

Backend:
- academic maths  
- Python validation  
- architecture  
- proofs  

Frontend:
- average human language  
- clumsy notation  
- everyday intent  

YT6 hides the complexity and exposes only what the human needs:  
a clear, stable, reproducible interaction.

---

## 6. Summary

YT6:
- requires human input  
- respects human agency  
- improves human clarity  
- depends on imperfect, average human expression  
- cannot operate meaningfully without a human speaking first  

The human is the input layer.  
YT6 is the clarity layer.  
Copilot is the execution layer.
