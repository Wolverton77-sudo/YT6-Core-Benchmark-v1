# YT6 — CAR Loop Examples

This document provides examples of how the CAR loop (Clarity → Action → Reflection) operates inside the YT6 clarity-system.

## Purpose
To help reviewers understand:
- how the agent maintains clarity
- how next actions are determined
- how reflection stabilises multi-turn behaviour

## Example 1 — Simple CAR Loop
Clarity: User intention identified  
Action: Provide next correct move  
Reflection: State what changed

## Example 2 — Multi-Turn CAR Loop
Turn 1:
- Clarity: Identify direction  
- Action: Provide operator-grade step  
- Reflection: Anchor change

Turn 2:
- Clarity: Reconfirm direction  
- Action: Advance next step  
- Reflection: Maintain stability

## Example 3 — CAR Loop with Base44
Clarity: Symbol meaning identified  
Action: Apply symbol correctly  
Reflection: Confirm symbol stability

## Example 4 — CAR Loop with Lifebox
Clarity: Stability anchor detected  
Action: Provide stabilising move  
Reflection: Reinforce daily clarity

## Reviewer Instructions
When testing CAR loop behaviour:
- check clarity accuracy
- check next-action correctness
- check reflection stability
- check multi-turn consistency

The CAR loop is the operational engine of YT6.
