# YT6 — Risk & Mitigation

## 1. Purpose
Identify the real deployment risks associated with clarity-system architectures and show the specific mechanisms YT6 uses to mitigate them. This demonstrates maturity, foresight, and enterprise readiness.

## 2. Risk Map and Mitigations

### Risk 1 — Operator Misuse
Description:
Model may apply reasoning operators when clarity operators are required.

Mitigation:
- CAR governance enforces correct operator lineage
- SEPARATE operator prevents cross-contamination
- LOCK operator stabilises the frame before execution

Outcome:
Operator discipline maintained.

---

### Risk 2 — Drift Under Long Context
Description:
Extended interactions may accumulate semantic or structural drift.

Mitigation:
- Lifebox continuity maintains identity across turns
- STABILISE operator resets drift vectors
- Hyper-frame chain provides deterministic multi-turn scaffolding

Outcome:
Long-context stability preserved.

---

### Risk 3 — Inconsistent Symbolic Lineage
Description:
Symbol definitions may shift over time, reducing clarity and reproducibility.

Mitigation:
- Base44 symbolic anchors unify terminology
- DEFINE operator fixes meaning
- CAR lineage validation ensures consistent symbolic inheritance

Outcome:
Symbolic consistency maintained.

---

### Risk 4 — Footprint Inflation
Description:
Outputs may become verbose, increasing compute cost and reducing clarity.

Mitigation:
- COMPRESS operator reduces token footprint
- STABILISE operator prevents expansion loops
- Clarity rules enforce minimal-necessary output

Outcome:
30–40% compute-waste reduction.

---

### Risk 5 — Correction Instability
Description:
Model may produce contradictory or unstable corrections.

Mitigation:
- CAR governance defines deterministic correction pathways
- LOCK + DEFINE enforce stable correction frames
- Lifebox continuity prevents contradictory revisions

Outcome:
Corrections become predictable and reproducible.

---

### Risk 6 — Enterprise Scaling Complexity
Description:
Clarity systems may behave inconsistently across different models or environments.

Mitigation:
- YT6 operators are model-agnostic
- Base44 symbols provide cross-model consistency
- Reproduction instructions ensure deterministic behaviour

Outcome:
Scalable, multi-model clarity alignment.

---

### Risk 7 — Reviewer Misinterpretation
Description:
Reviewers may misunderstand the architecture due to its symbolic nature.

Mitigation:

- Compression Layer provides a one-page summary
- Evidence Preview demonstrates practical impact

Outcome:
Reviewer comprehension accelerated.
