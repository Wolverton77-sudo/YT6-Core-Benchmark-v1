# YT6 — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is YT6?
YT6 is a human-side clarity protocol designed to reduce drift, increase stability, and support reproducible multi-turn clarity.

## What is Base44?
Base44 is a symbolic clarity alphabet used to anchor meaning, prevent drift, and maintain cross-turn stability.

## What is Lifebox?
Lifebox is the stability layer of YT6, supporting daily clarity, footprint moments, and personal clarity anchors.

## What is the CAR Loop?
CAR = Clarity → Action → Reflection.
It ensures predictable, stable multi-turn behaviour.
