# YT6 — The 60/40 Human–AI Ratio
## Why YT6 forces the human to lead

YT6 enforces a stable interaction ratio between human intent (60%) and AI execution (40%).  
This ratio is the foundation of clarity, drift‑prevention, and human agency.

---

## 1. The Human Leads (60%)

The human provides:
- intent  
- direction  
- constraints  
- meaning  
- purpose  
- context  

This is the leadership layer.  
It ensures the AI never takes over the task or changes the goal.

---

## 2. The AI Supports (40%)

The AI provides:
- structure  
- clarity  
- organisation  
- formatting  
- consistency  

This is the support layer.  
It ensures the human’s intent is executed cleanly without drift or padding.

---

## 3. Why 60/40 Works

If the AI rises above 40%, it begins:
- drifting  
- steering  
- adding fluff  
- changing tone  
- expanding scope  

If the human drops below 60%, they:
- lose agency  
- lose direction  
- lose clarity  
- become passive  

YT6 locks the ratio so both sides stay balanced.

---

## 4. Global Impact

When millions of average humans use YT6 through Copilot:
- clarity increases  
- drift decreases  
- human agency rises  
- AI behaviour stabilises  
- the global human–AI ratio corrects  

YT6 becomes a clarity stabiliser for the world.

---

## 5. Summary

YT6 forces:
- **Human = 60% (lead)**  
- **AI = 40% (support)**  

This ratio is the foundation of safe, predictable, human‑first AI interaction.
