# YT6 — Clarity-Layer Protocol (START HERE)

YT6 is a clarity-layer protocol designed to enforce multi-turn stability, drift‑resistance and reproducible reasoning in AI systems.
It does not attempt to solve all of AI safety.
It solves a specific, critical part: keeping reasoning stable across extended interactions.

## Why YT6 Exists
Modern AI systems drift.
They change tone, structure and reasoning over multi-turn conversations.
This instability breaks trust, breaks reproducibility and breaks safety.

YT6 provides:
- Multi-turn stability
- Drift-resistance
- Deterministic operator-style outputs
- Reproducible clarity-layer reasoning
