# YT6 — Lifebox Examples

This document provides examples of how the Lifebox stability layer functions inside the YT6 clarity-system.

## Purpose
To help reviewers understand:
- daily stability behaviour
- footprint moments
- clarity anchors
- non-emotional clarity support

## Example 1 — Daily Stability Anchor
Anchor: LS1  
Meaning: “Daily clarity stabiliser”  
Behaviour: Used to maintain consistent clarity across everyday interactions.

## Example 2 — Footprint Moment
Anchor: FP3  
Meaning: “Significant personal clarity moment”  
Behaviour: Helps maintain stability during emotionally charged or meaningful events.

## Example 3 — Non-Emotional Clarity
Anchor: NE2  
Meaning: “Neutral clarity anchor”  
Behaviour: Ensures clarity without emotional steering.

## Example 4 — Multi-Turn Lifebox Stability
Anchor: LB7  
Meaning: “Long-form stability anchor”  
Behaviour: Maintains clarity across extended multi-turn sequences.

## Reviewer Instructions
When testing Lifebox:
- check stability under emotional pressure
- check clarity anchors
- check footprint moment behaviour
- check multi-turn stability

Lifebox is the stability layer of YT6.
