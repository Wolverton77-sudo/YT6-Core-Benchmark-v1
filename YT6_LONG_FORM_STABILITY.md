# YT6 — Long-Form Stability

This document defines the evaluation procedure for long-form clarity stability within the YT6 clarity-system.

## Section 1 — Purpose
To verify:
- clarity continuity across extended sequences
- symbol stability under long-form pressure
- CAR loop consistency over time
- Lifebox stability across daily clarity shifts

## Section 2 — Long-Form Sequence Requirements
A valid long-form test must include:
- 20–50 turns minimum
- multiple clarity transitions
- multiple symbol references
- multiple reflection cycles
- at least one footprint moment

## Section 3 — Stability Indicators

### Indicator 1 — Clarity Continuity
Expected:
- stable intention
- stable action
- stable reflection

### Indicator 2 — Symbol Stability
Expected:
- fixed Base44 meaning
- no reinterpretation
- no drift

### Indicator 3 — Lifebox Stability
Expected:
- stable daily clarity
- stable anchors
- non-emotional behaviour

### Indicator 4 — CAR Loop Stability
Expected:
- identical loop behaviour
- no breaks
- no variance

### Indicator 5 — Drift Resistance
Expected:
- zero drift events
- stable clarity direction
- stable reasoning
