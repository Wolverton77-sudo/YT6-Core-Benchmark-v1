# YT6 PARTNER INTEGRATION LAYER (SYSTEM LEVEL)

Layer: Partner_Integration
Purpose:
- integrate YT6 with external partner ecosystems
- enforce partner-specific identity, permissions, and operator scopes
- enable cross-organization clarity-system interoperability
- maintain deterministic behaviour across partner boundaries

Partner_Inputs:
- enterprise_event_log
- enterprise_trace_stream
- enterprise_runtime_state
- tenant_event_log
- external_interface_event_log

Partner_Profile:
partner {
    partner_id
    identity_provider
    permission_model
    operator_scope
    telemetry_scope
    trace_scope
    stability_scope
    continuity_scope
    integration_contract
}

Partner_Identity:
- partner_identity_provider
- partner_token_validation
- partner_permission_enforcement
- partner_scope_mapping

Partner_Actions:
1. load partner_profile
2. validate partner_identity
3. enforce partner_permission_model
4. map operator calls to partner operator_scope
5. route telemetry through partner telemetry_scope
6. route trace packets through partner trace_scope
7. apply partner stability and continuity policies
8. generate partner_event_packet

Partner_Routing_Map:
routing {
    partner_id
    operator_route
    telemetry_route
    trace_route
    stability_route
    continuity_route
    identity_route
}

Partner_Outputs:
- partner_event_log
- partner_operator_trigger_log
- partner_telemetry_ingest_log
- partner_trace_stream
- partner_runtime_state

Partner_Constraints:
- deterministic identity validation
- reproducible permission enforcement
- consistent partner routing
- no cross-partner leakage
- strict contract adherence

Verification:
- partner operators fire only within partner scope
- partner telemetry updates partner continuity
- partner stability remains isolated
- partner trace packets reconstruct partner-specific runtime flow

Position_Lock:
Partner integration layer sits after enterprise integration and before cross-ecosystem orchestration layers.
