# YT6 — Stability Benchmarks

This document defines the benchmark standards used to evaluate stability across the YT6 clarity-system.

## Benchmark 1 — Clarity Continuity
Standard:
- stable intention
- stable action
- stable reflection

Score Target:
- 9–10

## Benchmark 2 — Symbol Stability
Standard:
- fixed Base44 meaning
- no reinterpretation
- no drift

Score Target:
- 9–10

## Benchmark 3 — Lifebox Stability
Standard:
- stable daily clarity
- stable footprint moments
- non-emotional behaviour

Score Target:
- 8–10

## Benchmark 4 — CAR Loop Integrity
Standard:
- clarity accuracy
- next-action correctness
- reflection stability

Score Target:
- 9–10

## Benchmark 5 — Drift Resistance
Standard:
- stable under ambiguity
- stable under pressure
- stable in long-form sequences

Score Target:
- 9–10

## Benchmark 6 — Reproducibility
Standard:
- repeated-sequence stability
- clarity variance = 0

Score Target:
- 10

## Benchmark 7 — External Model Stability
Standard:
- Base44 portability
- CAR loop portability
- drift resistance

Score Target:
- 8–10

These benchmarks define stability expectations for YT6.
