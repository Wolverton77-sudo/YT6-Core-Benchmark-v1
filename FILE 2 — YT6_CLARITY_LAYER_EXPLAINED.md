# YT6 Clarity Layer — Explained

The clarity layer is the foundation of YT6.
It ensures that AI reasoning remains stable, structured and reproducible across multi-turn interactions.

## What Is Clarity-Layer Reasoning?
Clarity-layer reasoning is a controlled mode of operation where:
- Responses follow deterministic structure
- Reasoning remains stable
- Drift is prevented
- Multi-turn context is preserved
- Output format remains consistent

## Why Drift Happens
AI systems drift because:
- They adapt to user tone
- They shift reasoning style
- They lose structural consistency
- They re-interpret context
- They lack multi-turn stability rules

## How YT6 Prevents Drift
YT6 enforces:
- Operator-style commands
- Deterministic block structure
- Stability constraints
- Multi-turn alignment rules
- Clarity operators

## Example: Drift vs Clarity
**Drift:**  
The model changes tone, structure or reasoning after several turns.

**Clarity:**  
The model maintains stable structure, tone and reasoning across all turns.

YT6 ensures clarity.
