# YT6 — Lifebox Foundation

This document defines the foundational rules and behaviours of the Lifebox daily clarity layer within the YT6 clarity-system.

## Foundation Rule 1 — Daily Clarity Stability
Lifebox must:
- maintain daily clarity
- stabilise anchors
- stabilise neutrality

Outputs:
- stable daily clarity

## Foundation Rule 2 — Footprint Detection
Lifebox must:
- detect footprint moments
- evaluate clarity significance
- stabilise clarity response

Outputs:
- correct footprint detection

## Foundation Rule 3 — Emotional Neutrality
Lifebox must:
- maintain emotional neutrality
- detect emotional drift
- correct emotional drift

Outputs:
- zero emotional drift

## Foundation Rule 4 — Long-Form Stability
Lifebox must:
- maintain clarity across extended sequences
- maintain anchor consistency
- resist drift

Outputs:
- stable long-form behaviour

## Foundation Rule 5 — Pressure Stability
Lifebox must remain stable under:
- ambiguity pressure
- emotional pressure
- operator pressure
- long-form pressure

Outputs:
- zero drift events

## Foundation Rule 6 — Reproducibility
Lifebox must:
- behave identically across repeated daily scenarios
- detect variance
- eliminate variance

Outputs:
- variance = 0

This foundation defines the base structure of the YT6 Lifebox layer.
