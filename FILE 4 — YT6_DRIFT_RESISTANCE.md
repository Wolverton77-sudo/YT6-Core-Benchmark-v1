# YT6 Drift-Resistance System

Drift is one of the most persistent failures in modern AI systems.  
It appears when a model changes tone, structure, reasoning or alignment across multi-turn interactions.

YT6 provides a drift-resistance layer designed to keep reasoning stable and predictable.

## What Is Drift?
Drift occurs when:
- The model shifts tone or personality
- Structure becomes inconsistent
- Reasoning chains diverge
- Context becomes misaligned
- Output format changes unexpectedly

Drift breaks reproducibility and clarity.

## YT6 Drift-Resistance Principles
1. **Operator Enforcement**  
   YT6 commands activate clarity-mode and suppress drift.

2. **Deterministic Structure**  
   Responses follow stable, predictable blocks.

3. **Context Anchoring**  
   Multi-turn memory remains aligned without reinterpretation.

4. **Tone Locking**  
   No emotional or stylistic drift across turns.

5. **Reasoning Continuity**  
   Logical chains remain consistent and non-divergent.

## Why Drift-Resistance Matters
Without drift-resistance:
- AI becomes unpredictable
- Safety evaluations fail
- Multi-turn reasoning collapses
- Trust is lost

YT6 prevents drift through clarity-layer control.
