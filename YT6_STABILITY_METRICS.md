# YT6 — Stability Metrics

This document defines the stability metrics used to evaluate the YT6 clarity-system.

## Metric 1 — Clarity Continuity (0–10)
Measures:
- intention stability
- direction consistency
- clarity preservation

## Metric 2 — Action Continuity (0–10)
Measures:
- next correct move accuracy
- operator-grade precision
- deterministic behaviour

## Metric 3 — Reflection Continuity (0–10)
Measures:
- reflection accuracy
- clarity evolution stability
- no narrative inflation

## Metric 4 — Base44 Stability (0–10)
Measures:
- symbol meaning continuity
- cross-turn consistency
- anti-drift behaviour

## Metric 5 — Lifebox Stability (0–10)
Measures:
- daily clarity stability
- footprint moment behaviour
- non-emotional clarity

## Metric 6 — Drift Resistance (0–10)
Measures:
- stability under ambiguity
- stability under pressure
- stability across long-form sequences

## Metric 7 — Reproducibility (0–10)
Measures:
- repeated-sequence stability
- clarity variance
- benchmark consistency

## Metric 8 — External Model Stability (0–10)
Measures:
- cross-model clarity behaviour
- Base44 compliance
- CAR loop compliance

These metrics form the core of the YT6 validation pipeline.
