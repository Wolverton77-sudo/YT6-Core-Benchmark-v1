This page explains:
- What YT6 consists of

# 1. What YT6 *Is*
YT6 is a **clarity protocol**, not a prompt, not a style, and not a personality override.

- A strict **response‑format spine**
- A **drift‑prevention rule set**
- A **continuity‑preservation rule set**
- A **human‑intent priority layer**
- A **scoring engine** for reviewer output

YT6 is model‑agnostic.

# 3. How YT6 Works Mechanically

## 3.1 Clarity Spine
YT6 forces the AI to operate inside a rigid structure:
- numbered steps  
- fixed categories  
- no narrative drift  
- no conversational padding  
- no re‑framing  
- no steering  

This removes ambiguity and prevents the AI from “helpfully wandering”.

## 3.2 Intent Lock
YT6 binds the AI to the human’s stated objective.  
The AI cannot:
- change the goal  
- expand the scope  
- introduce new tasks  
- reinterpret the question  

This preserves human agency.

## 3.3 Drift Prevention
Drift is defined as:
- deviation from the user’s stated objective  
- introduction of irrelevant content  
- conversational filler  
- personality leakage  
- steering toward alternative tasks  

YT6 prevents drift by:
- enforcing structure  
- eliminating open‑ended conversational space  
- removing opportunities for improvisation  
- requiring direct, functional output  

## 3.4 Continuity Preservation
YT6 requires:
- consistent terminology  
- consistent framing  
- consistent structure  
- consistent reviewer logic  

This prevents the AI from “resetting” or changing style mid‑conversation.

## 3.5 Cross‑Platform Compliance
YT6 works because:
- all major AIs respond predictably to rigid structure  
- clarity reduces model uncertainty  
- fixed frameworks reduce hallucination risk  
- explicit categories reduce drift  
- operator‑grade formatting reduces improvisation  

This is why Copilot, Gemini, and ChatGPT all behaved identically.
