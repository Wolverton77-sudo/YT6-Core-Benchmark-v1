# YT6 ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION LAYER (SYSTEM LEVEL)

Layer: Enterprise_Integration
Purpose:
- integrate YT6 with enterprise systems and identity boundaries
- enforce enterprise-grade permissions and operator scopes
- align telemetry, trace, stability, and continuity with enterprise policies
- ensure deterministic multi-tenant behaviour under enterprise governance

Enterprise_Inputs:
- tenant_event_log
- tenant_trace_stream
- tenant_runtime_state
- external_interface_event_log
- multi_node_runtime_state

Enterprise_Profile:
enterprise {
    enterprise_id
    identity_provider
    permission_model
    operator_policy
    telemetry_policy
    trace_policy
    stability_policy
    continuity_policy
}

Identity_Integration:
- enterprise_identity_provider
- enterprise_token_validation
- enterprise_permission_enforcement
- enterprise_scope_mapping

Enterprise_Actions:
1. load enterprise_profile
2. validate enterprise_identity
3. enforce enterprise_permission_model
4. map operator calls to enterprise operator_policy
5. route telemetry through enterprise telemetry_policy
6. route trace packets through enterprise trace_policy
7. apply enterprise stability and continuity policies
8. generate enterprise_event_packet

Enterprise_Routing_Map:
routing {
    enterprise_id
    operator_route
    telemetry_route
    trace_route
    stability_route
    continuity_route
    identity_route
}

Enterprise_Outputs:
- enterprise_event_log
- enterprise_operator_trigger_log
- enterprise_telemetry_ingest_log
- enterprise_trace_stream
- enterprise_runtime_state

Enterprise_Constraints:
- deterministic identity validation
- reproducible permission enforcement
- consistent enterprise routing
- no cross-enterprise leakage
- strict policy adherence

Verification:
- enterprise operators fire only within enterprise policy
- enterprise telemetry updates enterprise continuity
- enterprise stability remains isolated per enterprise
- enterprise trace packets reconstruct enterprise-specific runtime flow

Position_Lock:
Enterprise integration layer sits after multi-tenant and before partner-integration layers.
