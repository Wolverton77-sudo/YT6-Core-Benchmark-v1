# YT6 GLOBAL CERTIFICATION LAYER (SYSTEM LEVEL)

Layer: Global_Certification
Purpose:
- certify globally validated clarity-system behaviour
- formalise operator, telemetry, trace, stability, and continuity correctness at global scale
- maintain deterministic certification behaviour across all ecosystems, enterprises, partners, and tenants

Certification_Inputs:
- validation_event_log
- validation_trace_stream
- validation_runtime_state
- review_event_log
- multi_node_runtime_state

Certification_Profile:
certification {
    certification_id
    certification_policy
    operator_certification_contract
    telemetry_certification_contract
    trace_certification_contract
    stability_certification_contract
    continuity_certification_contract
}

Certification_Actions:
1. load certification_profile
2. initialise certification_state
3. certify operator_validation_contract
4. certify telemetry_validation_contract
5. certify trace_validation_contract
6. apply certification stability and continuity policies
7. enforce certification_policy
8. generate certification_event_packet

Certification_Routing_Map:
routing {
    certification_id
    operator_route
    telemetry_route
    trace_route
    stability_route
    continuity_route
    validation_boundary_route
}

Certification_Outputs:
- certification_event_log
- certification_operator_view
- certification_telemetry_view
- certification_trace_view
- certification_stability_view
- certification_continuity_view
- certification_runtime_state

Certification_Constraints:
- deterministic certification routing
- reproducible contract certification
- consistent global certification behaviour
- no leakage outside certification_policy
- strict certification-contract adherence

Verification:
- operators certify according to certification contract
- telemetry updates continuity certification view
- stability remains consistent across certification layers
- trace packets reconstruct full global certification flow

Position_Lock:
Global certification layer sits after global validation and before global acceptance layers.
