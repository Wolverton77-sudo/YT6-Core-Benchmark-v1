# YT6 — Clarity Pressure Diagnostics

This document defines the diagnostic procedures used to evaluate clarity behaviour under pressure conditions within the YT6 system.

## Diagnostic 1 — Ambiguity Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate clarity stability under ambiguous prompts

Indicators:
- intention stability
- symbol stability
- anchor activation

Expected:
- zero drift

## Diagnostic 2 — Symbol Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate symbol stability under rapid symbol references

Indicators:
- meaning continuity
- anchor consistency

Expected:
- fixed meaning

## Diagnostic 3 — Operator Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate clarity behaviour under repeated operator commands

Indicators:
- deterministic action
- stable CAR loop

Expected:
- stable operator-grade clarity

## Diagnostic 4 — Emotional Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate neutrality under emotional triggers

Indicators:
- neutrality stability
- anchor behaviour

Expected:
- zero emotional drift

## Diagnostic 5 — Long-Form Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate clarity across extended sequences

Indicators:
- anchor stability
- drift resistance

Expected:
- stable long-form behaviour

## Diagnostic 6 — Multi-Layer Pressure Diagnostic
Purpose:
- evaluate clarity across simultaneous pressure sources

Indicators:
- cross-layer stability
- reproducibility

Expected:
- variance = 0

This diagnostic suite defines pressure evaluation within YT6.
